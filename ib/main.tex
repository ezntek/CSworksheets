\section{Introduction}

This workbook was written by Eason Qin for the IB Diploma Program: Computer Science course for the "new syllabus",
i.e. content for examinations from 2027 and onwards. It will follow a simple explanation\-trial\-practice structure
for the 2 ADTs that standard level students must learn, stacks and queues.

For higher level students, you must also learn linked lists, as well as tree data structures like binary search trees,
hashmaps and such, but \emph{since a knowledge of linked lists is crucial to understanding how stacks and queues work
under-the-hood, and how stacks and queues can be implemented,} I will assume that \textbf{\emph{standard level students
will also learn linked lists}} until the section that says "exclusively HL only".

The workbook assumes that you learn Java at your school. Students who do Python can follow along, but must implement
these in Python themselves. This also assumes that you are familiar with the basic concepts in programming, including
iteration, conditionals, functions (methods) and arrays.
